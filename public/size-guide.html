Full custom measurements

For a Custom Race Suit, we need 23 individual measurements — chest, waistline, neck, sleeve length, inseam, and more — all in centimeters. The guide below shows exactly how to take each one.

  1. Download the measurement guide (photo instructions for each of the 23 points).
  2. Download the measurement form and fill in every field in centimeters.
  3. Have a second person take the measurements where possible.
  4. Add the completed form as a note at checkout, or reach out separately if you'd rather send it as a file.
